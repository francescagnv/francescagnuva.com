hi! I’m francesca. I was born and raised in nice, france. Growing up, I was obsessed with math and physics, but also philosophy and the arts. I skied competitively and sketched in my free time. After graduating from high school, I went to columbia (go lions!), where I majored in applied mathematics and also spent a lot of time in and around the history and visual arts departments. My two favorite math classes were Analysis and Optimization (MATH UN2500) and Complex Analysis (MATH UN3007). My senior applied mathematics thesis research (supervised by Chris Wiggins) focused on the evolution of bias and homophily in professional hierarchies and impact to the percentage of women rising to the upper echelons of certain industries (academia, finance, law, medicine). I worked in finance for a little over three years (investment banking at rothschild & co in new york / private equity at TA Associates in boston, where I sourced our investment in kline hill partners). I transitioned into tech when I began investing at hummingbird in 2025. After falling in love with san francisco, I joined audacious ventures’ investment team in SF in early 2026.

I love meeting talented engineers and entrepreneurs building at the frontier, which is one of the reasons I feel privileged to work in venture. I also love meeting new people in general, so please drop me a note (francesca@audacious.co!) if you’d like to grab coffee or go on a walk :)
