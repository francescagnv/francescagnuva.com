PHOTOS FOR YOUR MAP SPOTS
=========================

Drop a photo into this folder named after the spot and it shows up when you
click that pin on the map. Nothing to edit in the code.

  verjus.jpg      the photo for Verjus
  verjus-2.jpg    a second photo -- arrows appear so you can flip between them
  verjus-3.jpg    a third, and so on (up to 12 per spot)

.jpg, .jpeg and .png all work. Keep each under roughly 400kb so the popups stay
snappy. Numbering must be consecutive: if -2 is missing, -3 is never looked for.

Remember to commit this folder to GitHub along with index.html and 404.html.
